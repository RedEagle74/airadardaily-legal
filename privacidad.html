Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. What the app does

AIRadar Daily Publisher is a personal tool that connects to the TikTok account @airadardaily through TikTok Login Kit and uploads the owner's own videos through the TikTok Content Posting API.

2. Data we access

The app only accesses basic profile information of the account that the owner authorizes (such as the display name, needed to show which account will receive the video) and the access tokens that TikTok issues for that account. It does not access other users' data, the feed, direct messages or contacts.

3. How data is stored

The access tokens are stored on the owner's private server, protected with restricted file permissions. They are used only to upload the owner's videos and are never shown publicly.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, share or disclose any data to third parties. The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

5. Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the app permissions in his TikTok account settings. The stored tokens can also be deleted from the server.
